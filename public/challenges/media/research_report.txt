RECOVERED RESEARCH REPORT
Project: LUMEN-4
Laboratory Status: Decommissioned
Lead Researcher: Dr. Elias Varen

------------------------------------------------------------

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project LUMEN-4 investigated the stability of synthetic compounds under prolonged exposure to controlled environmental conditions.

The laboratory was equipped with temperature sensors, pressure monitors, light detectors, and an automated recording system. Each experiment was assigned a number, and samples were photographed before and after exposure.

The first experiments produced nothing unusual.

That changed during Experiment 17.

------------------------------------------------------------

2. EXPERIMENT 17

Sample L4-17 displayed a small change after exposure.

The automated sensors detected nothing unusual. The change was first noticed by comparing the sample with its original photograph.

The experiment was repeated several times.

Most attempts produced no change.

One produced the same anomaly.

The equipment was inspected and recalibrated. No faults were discovered.

The result remained inconsistent.

------------------------------------------------------------

3. OBSERVATIONS

Beginning with Experiment 21, additional observations were recorded manually.

The automated records appeared normal, but several manual measurements differed slightly from them.

A review of earlier experiments revealed similar discrepancies.

The older analysis notes contained repeated references to direction, magnitude, and a fixed reference point. At the time, these were considered ordinary descriptions of the measurements.

One researcher had written a short reminder beside the calculations:

"Values may change when the reference changes."

The note was never explained.

The differences were too small to affect the official results, so they had never been investigated.

Something else was unusual.

Several notes contained information unrelated to the experiments.

One described a corridor.

Another mentioned a light being switched off in an empty room.

A third described someone leaving the laboratory before an experiment had ended.

These details were removed from the working copy.

The original records were preserved.

------------------------------------------------------------

4. EXPERIMENT 36

Experiment 36 produced the same anomaly as Experiment 17.

This time, two independent instruments recorded the change.

The laboratory recording system also contained an additional entry between two normal measurements.

It was only a few characters long.

It did not correspond to any known system command.

The entry was removed from the working copy.

A backup was made before the original record was altered.

------------------------------------------------------------

5. FINAL WEEK

During the final week, access to the laboratory was restricted.

The official reason was equipment maintenance.

The equipment itself appeared to be functioning normally.

The research notes, however, became increasingly strange.

Some entries repeated information that had already been recorded.

Others contained unnecessary details.

The final handwritten observation was:

The measurements are correct.

The record is not.

No explanation followed.

------------------------------------------------------------

6. EXPERIMENT 42

Experiment 42 was the final recorded experiment.

The equipment was functioning normally.

The sample was prepared normally.

The chamber was sealed normally.

For the first eleven minutes, nothing unusual occurred.

At 11 minutes and 32 seconds, the light sensor recorded a brief interruption lasting less than one second.

No other instrument registered a change.

The experiment continued until completion.

The sample appeared unchanged.

The experiment was classified as inconclusive.

The classification was later crossed out.

No replacement was entered.

------------------------------------------------------------

7. FINAL NOTE

This section was not included in the original report.

It was found at the end of the recovered copy.

The handwriting does not match the rest of the document.

The note reads:

If you are reading this, then the report survived.

That was not supposed to happen.

Do not trust the parts that were written for you.

Read the record again.

Some values were shifted during the original analysis. The shift was repeated, but the reference remained the same.

There is information here that was never meant to be recorded.

Look carefully.

The project name was chosen for a reason.

------------------------------------------------------------

END OF RECOVERED REPORT
